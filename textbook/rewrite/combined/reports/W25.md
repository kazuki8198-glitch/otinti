## 4. 事実の確かめ（一次資料）と主張の札

- 米国の 14 CFR 91.123(a)「When a pilot is uncertain of an ATC clearance, that pilot shall immediately request clarification from ATC.」・14 CFR 1.1 の Ceiling（Cornell LII）。FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary の ROGER（「I have received all of your last transmission. It should not be used to answer a question requiring a yes or a no answer.」）。
